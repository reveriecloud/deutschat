German A1 for work

12 weeks, about 45 minutes a day. Tick each week when done. Tap any yellow button to hear it spoken.

0 of 12 weeks

Daily 45 minutes

  • 20 min lesson: DW Nicos Weg (A1) or the week’s topic.
  • 10 min flashcards: Anki or the Work phrases tab. Always learn nouns with der/die/das.
  • 10 min speak aloud: repeat phrases, shadow Easy German videos.
  • 5 min write 3 sentences about your workday.

German is mostly spelled the way it sounds. Learn these rules once and you can read almost any word. Rough English hints are approximate; listen and copy.
